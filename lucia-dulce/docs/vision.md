# Vision

## Product Vision
Lucia Dulce is a premium Filipino bakery brand built to bring celebration, warmth, and joy to every customer through handcrafted cakes, pastries, and breads. The website serves as the digital face of the brand — a luxury experience that drives trust, emotional connection, and customer orders.

## Target Users
- **Primary**: Individuals and families in Davao City, Tagum, and Digos seeking premium cakes for celebrations (birthdays, weddings, graduations, anniversaries).
- **Secondary**: Gift buyers looking for bento cakes and ensaymada pasalubong.
- **Tertiary**: Daily merienda customers who visit physical stores.

## Core Problem
The original luciadulce.com website was a basic informational site that underrepresented the quality and warmth of the brand. Customers couldn't easily browse products, understand pricing, or be inspired to place orders online.

## Product Goals
1. Drive online orders and inquiries via a clear CTA flow.
2. Build brand prestige and trust through luxury UI/UX.
3. Help customers find the nearest store location easily.
4. Showcase the full product catalog with beautiful photography.
5. Tell the Lucia Dulce story emotionally to build loyalty.

## Success Metrics
- Increased order inquiries via the Contact page.
- Lower bounce rate vs. old site.
- Customers can find any product within 2 clicks.
- Mobile experience rated highly (primary access device in PH).

## MVP Scope
- Homepage (hero, categories, brand story, testimonials, locations preview)
- Full Menu page with filters by category
- About page with brand story
- Locations page with all 9 store branches
- Contact/Order page with inquiry form
