# Business Rules

## User Roles
- **Customer**: Browse products, view locations, submit order inquiries
- **Admin** (Phase 2): Manage products, upload photos, edit prices, view orders

## Product Categories
1. **Yema Cakes** — Signature product, available in Small/Round/Rectangle sizes
2. **Premium Cakes** — Fixed sizes, sold whole
3. **Bento Cakes** — 4-inch individual cakes, ₱200–₱250
4. **Ensaymada** — Sold individually, ₱30–₱40
5. **Celebration Cakes** — Customizable, round or rectangle
6. **Pastries & Rolls** — Breads, cookies, delicacies

## Pricing Rules
- All prices are in Philippine Pesos (₱)
- Prices shown are for standard sizes
- Custom cakes require inquiry via Contact form
- No online payment in MVP (inquire-to-order model)

## Ordering Process (MVP)
1. Customer browses menu
2. Customer clicks "Order Now" → directed to Contact page
3. Customer fills order inquiry form (name, product, occasion, message, contact)
4. Lucia Dulce team responds via phone/message

## Location Rules
- 9 branches across Davao City (3), Tagum (3), Digos (2), Davao Del Sur (1)
- Each branch has its own phone number
- Hours vary by branch (4am–9pm for GL Bakeshop/Sta. Ana early-morning pandesal)

## Content Rules
- All product descriptions must reflect Filipino bakery culture and warmth
- Brand voice: warm, celebratory, family-focused, premium but accessible
- Tagline: "Every Day is a Celebration!"
- No external competitor mentions
